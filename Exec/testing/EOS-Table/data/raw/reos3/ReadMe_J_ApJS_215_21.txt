   <Astrophys. J. Suppl. Ser., 215, 21 (2014)>
   =2014ApJS..215...21B    (SIMBAD/NED BibCode)

Abstract:
    We present new equations of state (EOSs) for hydrogen and helium
    covering a wide range of temperatures from 60K to 10^7^K and densities
    from 10^-10^g/cm^3^ to 10^3^g/cm^3^. They include an extended set of
    ab initio EOS data for the strongly correlated quantum regime with an
    accurate connection to data derived from other approaches for the
    neighboring regions. We compare linear mixing isotherms based on our
    EOS tables with available real mixture data. A first important
    astrophysical application of this new EOS data is the calculation of
    interior models for Jupiter and comparison with recent results.
    Second, mass-radius relations are calculated for Brown Dwarfs (BDs)
    which we compare with predictions derived from the widely used EOS of
    Saumon, Chabrier, and van Horn. Furthermore, we calculate interior
    models for typical BDs with different masses, namely, Corot-3b,
    Gliese-229b, and Corot-15b, and the giant planet KOI-889b. The
    predictions for the central pressures and densities differ by up to
    10% dependent on the EOS used.
